I turn lightinto sound.

A short laser pulse heats whatever absorbs it. The heat makes it expand for an instant, and that launches an ultrasound wave. Listen at the surface and you can rebuild what is inside. That is photoacoustic imaging, and it is what I work on at Boğaziçi's Photoacoustic Laboratory.
